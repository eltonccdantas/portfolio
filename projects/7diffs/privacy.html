    7diffs collects no data

    Read how 7diffs keeps your comparisons private by processing everything locally on your device.

    Last updated: June 2, 2026

    🔒 Summary: 7diffs does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All text comparison and file processing happen entirely on your device.

    1. Data Collection

    7diffs collects no personal data whatsoever. There is no telemetry, analytics, error reporting, advertising SDK, or tracking of any kind in the application.

    We do not know who you are, what texts you compare, or how you use the app.

    2. Offline Processing

    7diffs is designed to work offline. All diff calculations are performed locally on your machine. No content is uploaded or shared with any server or third party.

    The application does not require an internet connection to compare text or files. No network requests are made by the app during normal use.

    Files you open remain on your device and are processed only in memory while the app is running.

    3. Permissions

    7diffs requests only the permissions necessary to access files you choose to open:

    macOS

    • File system access: to read the files you select for comparison.

    Android

    • File access: to open text files from your device storage.

    iOS

    • File access: via the system file picker only, with no broad storage access.

    No permission is used for any purpose other than those described above.

    4. Children’s Privacy

    7diffs does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your jurisdiction). The app does not contain features directed at children that require special data handling under laws such as COPPA, GDPR-K, or similar regulations.

    5. Third-Party Services

    7diffs does not integrate with third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or cloud storage providers.

    The app uses open-source libraries and runs entirely on your device. No external connections are made during normal use.

    6. Data Security

    Because 7diffs never transmits or stores your text or files outside your device, the risk of a privacy breach originating from 7diffs is essentially zero. Your comparisons remain under your control at all times.

    7. Your Rights

    Because 7diffs collects no personal data, there is no stored data to access, correct, export, or delete. All processed files remain on your device and can be managed using your operating system tools.

    8. Changes to This Policy

    If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be published alongside the app release. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the revision date.

    Continued use of 7diffs after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
